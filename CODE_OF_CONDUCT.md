
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Hello World is an educational project for people at different stages of their development journey.

We want everyone to feel comfortable learning, asking questions, sharing knowledge and contributing.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Welcome beginners and different experience levels.
- Be patient when explaining technical concepts.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Accept reasonable criticism.
- Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful language.
- Bullying or intimidation.
- Deliberately humiliating people for being beginners.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behaviour that prevents constructive participation.

## Reporting Concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Include relevant details and supporting context where possible. Reports will be handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and keep project spaces constructive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other spaces where participants represent Hello World.

## Final Principle

Everyone starts somewhere. Treat other people with the respect you would expect when learning something new.
